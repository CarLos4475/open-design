Who We Are

Engineers who understand
the game

We're a team of software engineers, data scientists, and sports technologists. We've shipped products used by professional leagues, national federations, and consumer fitness platforms across three continents.

Founded in 2019, Code Stone was born from a simple observation: the sports industry runs on legacy systems and spreadsheets. Athletes generate terabytes of performance data. Fans expect real-time digital experiences. Clubs need tools that work on game day, not just in demos.

We bridge that gap. Every product we build is grounded in how sport actually works — training cycles, match-day operations, fan behavior, and the business models that sustain teams and organizations.
